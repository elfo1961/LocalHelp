# Usage Example — Pool Cleaning (Client & Provider POV)

This example shows how LocalHelp connects a client who needs pool‑cleaning with a provider who offers that service.

---

## Client POV — “I need someone to clean my pool”

### Scenario
A user needs help cleaning their pool and wants to find someone nearby.

### Goal
Create a help request and receive offers from service providers.

### Journey

1. The user opens LocalHelp and selects their preferred language.
2. They navigate to **Help Requests**.
3. They tap **Create a new request**.
4. They enter:
   - “Pool cleaning needed”
   - A short description
   - Preferred time
   - Optional photos
5. They choose a category such as “Cleaning” or “Outdoor Services”.
6. They publish the request.
7. Nearby service providers see the request in their feed.
8. Providers can message or candidate themselves.
9. The user reviews candidates, checks profiles, and starts a chat.
10. They agree on details and schedule the service.

### Outcome
The user quickly receives offers from local pool‑cleaning providers.

---

## Provider POV — “I’m a pool cleaner looking for clients”

### Scenario
A pool‑cleaning professional wants to find clients who already need help.

### Goal
Browse existing help requests and candidate themselves for relevant jobs.

### Journey

1. The provider logs into LocalHelp.
2. They navigate to **Help Requests**.
3. They search for keywords like “pool” or “cleaning”.
4. Matching client requests appear in their local area.
5. They tap a request such as **“Need someone to clean my pool”**.
6. They review:
   - client description
   - location
   - preferred time
   - any photos
7. They tap **Candidate Myself** (or “Offer Help”).
8. They send a short message introducing themselves:
   - “Hi, I’m a pool‑cleaning professional. I can help you today.”
9. The client receives the message and can view the provider’s profile.
10. They chat, agree on details, and schedule the job.

### Outcome
The provider easily finds clients who already need pool‑cleaning services and can apply directly to their requests.
